# 04.1 PRD

El PRD es el documento que junta todo lo que definiste (problema, personas, jobs, stories, MVP) en una sola fuente de verdad, lista para entregar a un equipo de desarrollo. Si hiciste todo en orden, no es trabajo nuevo: es ensamblaje.

<aside>
💡

Dos errores a evitar. Repetir el brief sin incorporar los aprendizajes del research, y llenarlo de soluciones de diseño (el PRD define el qué y el porqué, no el cómo visual). La IA es útil para armar el primer borrador, pero las decisiones (qué entra, qué prioridad) las tomas tú.

</aside>

## Tu PRD

Version: 1.0 (MVP)

Product: My Local Guide - App to find experiencies that are authenthicly local

State: Approved to be designed and developed

Last update: 10/07/2026

### 1. Resumen ejecutivo

MLG is an app to find authenthic experiecies approved by locals while you plan or you’re already travelling.

The difference between other platforms or SM is the actual authenticity check made by locals and experts that validate the experience, avoiding to users to be scammed or fall into tourist traps.

### 2. Problema

- Lack of checks on experiences
- Hard to find an authenthic local experience
- reservation planning hard to make when speak different languages
- restrictions in cancelling experiences
- price ranges

Research (25 people surveyed and 6 people deeply interviewed) validate that people fear to be scammed, shifted the preferencies to unique experiencies that are not easy to find on sm or other apps.

50% of them want to see photos and reviews that are actually real, not made up.

48% wants to be able to cancel the experience without problems

### 3. Usuarios objetivo

- 24 - Master's student
- Do research to find something authenthic and unique
- Main necessity: find authenthic experiences approved by local people
- Priority: authenthicity, one of a kind
- Main user

### 5. Alcance del MVP

**MH**

1. Research tab (main function)
2. experience card (to show a preview)
3. map to identify locsations (location finder to make it easier to reach)
4. certification by local people (to guarantee quality)
5. dm with host (to ask infos)
6. booking service ( reservations make it easier to organize everything else when abroad)
7. payment service (allow multiple methods)

## SH

1. Past experiences list (useful to remind, suggest)
2. saving experience for later (if not sure, to help not to loose the experience)
3. Comparison section (compare similar experiences)
4. feedback section (to have an idea of othher customers experience )
